## Failure Interpretation and Recommendation

The fixed-affinity protocol removed most of protocol v3's comparator drift: the
result improved from 40/54 to 50/54, every physical cell still passes, and all
buffered file-backed cells now pass. The remaining failures are concentrated in
the fastest eight-worker vector paths, where scheduling and per-key CPU work
dominate rather than storage I/O. Two failures are throughput-only, one is
latency-only, and one narrowly fails both (`0.786624` throughput and `1.256497`
p95 ratio).

T263 completed that focused diagnostic in one pinned dual-version process. All
four aggregate throughput and p95 ratios passed; paired medians also passed, AB/BA
order effects changed direction by cell, and every operation remained on CPUs
12–19. The complete evidence is in
`t263-protocol-v4-20260807-214009.md`. It does not prove a stable candidate-only
hot-path regression and therefore does not justify production optimization or a
threshold change.

The safest next step is an explicitly approved protocol-v5 complete capture that
counterbalances every pre-feature/candidate pair inside one pinned process. Do
not promote this failed attempt or reuse the four focused diagnostic cells as
acceptance evidence.
