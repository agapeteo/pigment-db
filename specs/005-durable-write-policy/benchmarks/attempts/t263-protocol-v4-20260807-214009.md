# T263 Alternating Same-Process Diagnostic

Status: **COMPLETE — no stable candidate-only regression reproduced**

This focused diagnostic remeasured only protocol v4's four failed cells. The
pre-feature and candidate crates were linked under separate names into one release
binary, and every cell ran as eleven counterbalanced pairs: baseline/candidate for
even pairs and candidate/baseline for odd pairs. Five pairs per cell were warmed
in the same alternating order before measurement.

No acceptance evidence was overwritten or promoted. This diagnostic does not
change protocol v4's failed `50/54` verdict.

## Conclusion and Recommendation

This diagnostic does not reproduce a stable candidate-only buffered regression.
Every aggregate median passes, candidate throughput is faster in 4–5 of 11 pairs
per cell rather than consistently slower, and order effects change direction by
cell. The extreme ratios in both directions, despite exact process affinity,
identify residual within-set scheduling/frequency variance as the dominant cause
of protocol v4's four failures.

Do not optimize production code or relax thresholds from this evidence. The
safest next acceptance protocol is a protocol-v5 complete capture that links the
pre-feature and candidate implementations into one pinned process and
counterbalances every matching baseline/candidate pair. Physical
reference/candidate pairs should use the same counterbalancing while retaining
their per-operation rendezvous. Protocol v5 must recapture the complete 36
buffered and 18 physical matrices; the focused T263 rows are diagnostic only and
cannot be promoted.
