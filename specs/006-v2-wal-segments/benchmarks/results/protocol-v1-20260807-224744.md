## Interpretation and recommendation

The median results do not show a sustained throughput regression: the candidate is within 0.2% of baseline for eight-worker key/value writes and faster in the other two eight-worker medians. The low individual ratios are nevertheless real gate failures and are concentrated in the noisier concurrent workload.

Do not merge on this result and do not retroactively weaken SC-005. Prepare a diagnostic protocol that runs only the three eight-worker cells with longer samples in one pinned process, captures per-pair order and system interference, and preserves this failed attempt. If the longer diagnostic reproduces a candidate-specific lower tail, profile and optimize that path before rerunning the original acceptance matrix. If it instead demonstrates short-sample scheduler or I/O jitter, revise the future protocol prospectively with an explicit minimum sample duration, then perform a fresh acceptance capture under the revised contract.
